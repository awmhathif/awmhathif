## How I work

I don’t really follow a fixed learning path. I usually learn by making the thing I want to exist.

That has led to hundreds of small prototypes, larger Android projects, AI experiments, browser tools, creative interfaces, and plenty of unfinished ideas that taught me something anyway.

GitHub is becoming the public record of that side of my work.
