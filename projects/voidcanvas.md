# VoidCanvas

**A full-stack AI creation platform built around creative workflows rather than a single one-shot generator.**

VoidCanvas is one of my larger web projects and represents a different side of my work from the small experiments in CodeCanvas.

## What I was trying to build

The idea was to make AI creation feel more like a workspace: somewhere to move between ideas, generation, refinement, and different creative tools without every task feeling disconnected.

## Development scope

The private production repository is structured as a full-stack project with separate frontend and backend areas, Supabase integration, tests, and supporting project tooling.

A lot of the work has been less about making a single impressive screen and more about making many moving pieces behave like one product: state, generation flows, UI, persistence, backend behavior, testing, and iteration.

## Why it matters to me

VoidCanvas pushed me further into building larger systems instead of isolated prototypes. It combines the visual/creative side people already associate with @awmhathif with the software side I’m making more visible now.

The production source remains private.
