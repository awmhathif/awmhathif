# Neon Notes

**A local-first Android notes app built around speed, privacy, customization, and expressive UI.**

Neon Notes started as a personal attempt to make a notes app that felt less generic and more useful in everyday use. It has grown into one of my main Android projects.

## What it includes

- notes, tasks, lists, subtasks, and checklists
- reminders and quick capture
- Android widgets and a quick-settings capture tile
- backup, import, and export tools
- recycle bin and app lock
- multiple themes, note colors, editor customization, and custom fonts
- offline-first behavior with no account requirement

## What I focused on

The main challenge was not adding as many features as possible. It was keeping the app fast and coherent while letting notes, tasks, capture, widgets, reminders, and customization feel like parts of the same system.

I also spent a lot of time on the visual side: interaction details, colors, motion, compact layouts, and making the app feel distinct without sacrificing usability.

## Why it matters to me

Neon Notes is one of the projects where I stopped thinking in terms of a small experiment and started thinking more seriously about product design, performance, persistence, Android behavior, edge cases, and long-term iteration.

The production source remains private for now, but the app itself is public.
